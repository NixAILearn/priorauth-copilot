# Riverbend Orthopedics: Office Visit

**Patient:** Odile Strathmore · **DOB:** 03/15/1960 · **Date of visit: 09/07/2026**
**Provider:** Dr. Pell Valcourt, Orthopedic surgeon · **Referring:** Dr. Dashiell Kittredge

## Chief complaint

Right knee pain with mechanical symptoms.

## HPI

Mr. Strathmore is a 66 y/o M with right knee pain for about two years, gradually worse. Pain is medial, aching, worse on stairs and after walking. The knee gives way on stairs and when stepping off a curb, about twice a week. He denies the knee getting stuck. 

Treatment so far: naproxen and a supervised strengthening program starting 05/22/2026; 13 therapy visits through 08/28/2026, with modest gains but persistent pain. Naproxen stopped when therapy ended.

Function: walks about 15 to 20 minutes before needing to rest, uses the railing on stairs, and has stopped longer outings with family because of the knee. Sleep is sometimes disturbed by aching after a busy day.

## Review of systems

Constitutional: no fever, chills or weight loss. CV: no chest pain. Resp: no shortness of breath. GI: no bleeding. Skin: no rash or wounds over the knee. Neuro: no numbness or weakness.  All other systems reviewed and negative.

## Past medical history

- Primary osteoarthritis, right knee
- Essential hypertension

## Past surgical history

- No prior knee surgery on either side
- Appendectomy, childhood

## Medications

Reconciled today; see the medication list.

## Allergies

NKDA.

## Social history

Retired. Lives with spouse in a one-story home. Never smoker. Rare alcohol.

## Family history

Mother with knee osteoarthritis. Father with hypertension.

## Physical examination

**Vitals:** BP 128/76, HR 76.

General: well appearing, in no distress.
Gait: mildly antalgic on the right.
Right knee: small effusion, medial joint line tenderness, patellofemoral crepitus. Skin intact, no warmth or redness. Range of motion: full extension to 125 degrees of flexion. Ligaments stable to varus, valgus, Lachman and drawer testing.
Opposite knee: no effusion, mild crepitus.
Hips: painless motion.
Neurovascular: sensation intact; pedal pulses 2+.

## Imaging

Radiographs of the right knee from 08/24/2026 and MRI from 08/12/2026 reviewed with the patient.

## Assessment

Right knee osteoarthritis with giving-way episodes from quadriceps weakness and pain, and a cartilage lesion on MRI, after more than 12 weeks of conservative care.

## Plan

1. Discussed arthroscopic debridement of the right knee for the cartilage lesion, with realistic expectations: the procedure treats the mechanical problem, and arthritis pain may persist.
2. Risks reviewed: infection, bleeding, blood clots, stiffness, persistent pain and the need for further surgery.
3. Request prior authorization from Northwind Health Plan.
4. Continue acetaminophen as needed and home exercises until surgery.

## Discussion

We reviewed the imaging together using a knee model. The patient asked about recovery: most people walk the same day with crutches for a few days, return to light activity within two to three weeks, and continue strengthening exercises afterward. Questions were answered and the patient wishes to proceed.

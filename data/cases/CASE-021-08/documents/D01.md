## Study type and indication

This was an attended in-laboratory diagnostic polysomnogram. Indication: snoring, witnessed pauses in breathing and daytime sleepiness (Epworth Sleepiness Scale 13/24). No prior sleep study on file.

## Methods

Standard montage: EEG (frontal, central and occipital leads referenced to the contralateral mastoid), bilateral EOG, chin and bilateral anterior tibialis EMG, single-lead ECG, nasal pressure transducer, oronasal thermistor, thoracic and abdominal effort belts, pulse oximetry, body position sensor and synchronized video. A registered technologist attended the full study. Scoring followed current AASM scoring rules; hypopneas scored using the 4% oxygen desaturation rule (a 30% or greater drop in airflow for at least 10 seconds with a desaturation of at least 4%). Apneas were classified by respiratory effort.

## Oxygenation

Awake baseline SpO2 94%. Mean SpO2 in sleep 91%. Oxygen nadir 82%, in REM sleep. Time with SpO2 under 88%: 14.2 minutes.

## Cardiac and limb

Sinus rhythm with cyclical heart rate variation around events. No significant arrhythmia. Periodic limb movement index 1.1 per hour.

## Technologist comments

Ms. Valcourt arrived on time and completed the pre-study questionnaire. Sleep was "about the same as at home" on the post-study questionnaire. No equipment problems. 

## Interpretation

1. Obstructive sleep apnea (G47.33), moderate to severe by AHI, with predominantly obstructive events. 
2. Oxyhemoglobin desaturation with a nadir of 82%.
3. Fragmented sleep with an elevated arousal index.
4. No significant periodic limb movements.

## Recommendations

- Positive airway pressure therapy, by titration or auto-titrating CPAP per the referring physician.
- Positional measures as an adjunct.
- Counsel on drowsy driving.
- Follow up in sleep clinic.

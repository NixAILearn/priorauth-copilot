## Study type and indication

This was an attended in-laboratory diagnostic polysomnogram performed for loud snoring, witnessed apneas reported by his spouse, unrefreshing sleep and daytime sleepiness (Epworth Sleepiness Scale 13/24). Pt has treated HTN and hyperlipidemia. No prior sleep study on file.

## Methods

Standard montage: EEG (F4-M1, C4-M1, O2-M1), bilateral EOG, chin and bilateral anterior tibialis EMG, ECG (modified lead II), nasal pressure transducer, oronasal thermistor, thoracic and abdominal RIP belts, pulse oximetry, body position sensor and synchronized video. A registered polysomnographic technologist was present throughout. Scoring followed current AASM scoring rules; hypopneas scored using the 4% oxygen desaturation rule (a 30% or greater drop in airflow lasting at least 10 seconds with a desaturation of at least 4%). Apneas were scored as obstructive, central or mixed by effort channels.

## Interpretation

1. Moderate-to-severe obstructive sleep apnea (G47.33). Overall AHI 38.0 events/hour, predominantly obstructive events with few central or mixed apneas; central and mixed apneas together are under 3% of all events.
2. Associated oxyhemoglobin desaturation with nadir 79%.
3. Sleep fragmentation with elevated arousal index.
4. No significant periodic limb movements.

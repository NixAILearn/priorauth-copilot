# Sleep Medicine Follow-up

**Patient:** Dashiell Iverholt · **DOB:** 07/22/1958 · **Date of service: 08/27/2026**
**Provider:** Dr. Hollis Valcourt · **Location:** Cedar Hollow Sleep Center

Pt seen in person in clinic today for follow-up of OSA and CPAP intolerance.

## Interval history

68 y/o M with moderate-to-severe OSA (AHI 38.0 events/hour on attended PSG 03/12/2026). CPAP was started on 04/10/2026 with an auto-CPAP and full face mask. He had mask discomfort and persistent air leak from the start, with dry mouth and pressure marks on the bridge of the nose. He was refit with a nasal pillow mask on 05/08/2026, and humidification was increased. Leak improved slightly but he continued to remove the mask after a few hours. Despite supplier outreach and a trial of a chin strap discussion, he stopped using CPAP in mid-June and has not used it since. Compliance download for 05/10/2026 to 06/08/2026 reviewed, showing low use.

He still snores loudly per his wife, wakes unrefreshed and dozes when reading in the evening. ESS today 12/24. Denies drowsy driving. No cataplexy, sleep paralysis or hypnagogic hallucinations; no symptoms suggesting narcolepsy. No restless legs symptoms.

DISE 06/04/2026 with Dr. Marchetti-Lowe reviewed: complete anteroposterior collapse at the velum, partial lateral wall and tongue base, no concentric collapse.

## Plan

Discussed options again: further mask trials (he declines), oral appliance (he wants a more durable solution and has partial dentures), positional therapy alone (not adequate given lateral events), and upper airway stimulation. Given CPAP failure, favorable DISE and BMI in range, I recommend hypoglossal nerve stimulation. Referral placed to Dr. Kestrel Marchetti-Lowe, ENT, for implant consultation. Continue weight management; follow up in sleep clinic 3 months after implant activation for titration.

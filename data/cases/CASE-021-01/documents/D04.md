# Lakemont ENT Associates: Operative Report

**Patient:** Dashiell Iverholt · **DOB:** 07/22/1958 · **MRN:** SYN-0001
**Procedure date: 06/04/2026**
**Surgeon:** Dr. Kestrel Marchetti-Lowe · **Anesthesia:** MAC, propofol infusion

**Procedure:** Drug-induced sleep endoscopy (DISE), flexible fiberoptic nasopharyngolaryngoscopy under sedation.

**Pre-op dx:** Obstructive sleep apnea (G47.33), CPAP intolerance.
**Post-op dx:** Same.

## Indication

68 y/o M with moderate-to-severe OSA on PSG 03/12/2026, struggling with CPAP since early April because of mask discomfort and leak. Referred by Dr. Valcourt for airway evaluation ahead of possible HGNS candidacy.

## Description

Pt brought to the procedure suite and positioned supine on a pillow as for sleep. Topical oxymetazoline and lidocaine to the R naris. Propofol titrated by anesthesia to a sedation level with snoring and observed obstructive events, BIS in the 50s to 60s. Scope passed through the R nasal cavity without difficulty. Multiple obstructive cycles observed at each level before grading. Jaw thrust maneuver performed at the end.

## Findings (VOTE)

| Level | Degree | Pattern |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Velum | Complete (2) | Anteroposterior |
| Oropharynx (lateral walls) | Partial (1) | Lateral |
| Tongue base | Partial (1) | Anteroposterior |
| Epiglottis | None (0) | n/a |

Velum: complete anteroposterior collapse with each obstructive event; there is no complete concentric collapse at the level of the velum on any of the observed cycles. Lateral pharyngeal walls show partial inward movement only. Tongue base partial AP narrowing that improves with jaw thrust. Epiglottis stable. Tonsils small, grade 1, not contributing.

Nasal cavity: mild septal deviation to L, inferior turbinates mildly hypertrophic, airway patent.

## Complications

None. EBL nil. Pt recovered in PACU and was discharged home with escort.

## Impression

Multilevel partial and complete AP collapse with complete AP collapse at the velum and no concentric pattern. Anatomy is favorable for upper airway stimulation from a DISE standpoint.

## Plan

Results discussed with pt and spouse in recovery. Continue CPAP efforts and troubleshooting with the sleep team for now. Return to ENT clinic for surgical consultation if CPAP cannot be tolerated.

# Lakemont ENT Associates: Operative Report

**Patient:** Jory Quarrington · **DOB:** 10/17/1962
**Procedure date: 03/15/2026**
**Surgeon:** Dr. Dashiell Fenwick-Osei · **Anesthesia:** monitored anesthesia care, propofol infusion

**Procedure:** Drug-induced sleep endoscopy (DISE), flexible nasopharyngolaryngoscopy under sedation.
**Pre-op dx:** Obstructive sleep apnea (G47.33), intolerance of positive airway pressure.

## Description

Pt positioned supine with a pillow as for sleep. Topical decongestant and anesthetic to the left naris. Propofol titrated to snoring with observed obstructive events. Scope passed without difficulty and several obstructive cycles were watched at each level before grading. Jaw thrust done at the end. Oxygen saturation was monitored throughout and stayed above safe limits with brief dips during events.

## Findings (VOTE)

Velum: complete anteroposterior collapse; there is no complete concentric collapse at the level of the velum.

| Level | Degree | Pattern |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oropharynx (lateral walls) | Partial (1) | Lateral |
| Tongue base | Partial (1) | Anteroposterior |
| Epiglottis | None (0) | n/a |

Tonsils small and not contributing. Nasal cavity patent.

## Complications

None. Recovered in PACU and discharged home with an escort.

## Impression

Multilevel collapse without a concentric pattern at the soft palate. Airway findings are favorable for upper airway stimulation.

# Lakemont Regional Hospital: MRI Report

**Patient:** Ines Galbrecht · **DOB:** 01/03/1950 · **Exam date:** 08/21/2026
**Exam:** MRI right forefoot without and with contrast
**Ordering:** Dr. Dashiell Northcote · **Radiologist:** Dr. Nadir Duvane

## Clinical history

76-year-old man with diabetes and a right plantar first metatarsal head ulcer, ongoing for several weeks. Evaluate soft tissues and bone.

## Technique

Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the right forefoot on a 1.5 T system: sagittal T1 and STIR, axial T1, T2 fat-saturated and proton density, coronal T1 and T2 fat-saturated, and post-contrast fat-saturated T1 images in three planes after intravenous gadolinium. Renal function checked before contrast. A marker was placed on the skin at the ulcer.

## Comparison

Right foot radiographs from the primary care office, three views, earlier this summer: no fracture, mild degenerative change at the first metatarsophalangeal joint.

## Findings

**Skin and soft tissues:** A skin defect is present at the right plantar first metatarsal head at the marker, about 2.5 cm in length, with surrounding subcutaneous edema and mild enhancement consistent with granulation tissue and reactive change. No discrete abscess or drainable fluid collection. No soft tissue gas. Mild diffuse subcutaneous edema over the dorsum of the forefoot, nonspecific, likely dependent.

**Tendons:** The flexor hallucis longus and brevis tendons are intact without tenosynovitis. The extensor hallucis longus is intact. The flexor and extensor tendons of the lesser toes are normal. Mild fluid along the peroneus longus at the cuboid tunnel, within normal limits.

**Plantar plate and sesamoids:** The first metatarsophalangeal plantar plate is intact. The medial and lateral sesamoids are normal in position. There is mild sesamoid degenerative change without fragmentation.

**Joints:** Mild degenerative change of the first metatarsophalangeal joint with a small dorsal osteophyte and small joint effusion. The lesser metatarsophalangeal joints are normal. Tarsometatarsal joints aligned without effusion. No neuropathic joint changes in the midfoot.

**Muscles:** Mild fatty atrophy of the intrinsic foot muscles, typical of neuropathy. No intramuscular collection.

**Bones:** Normal marrow signal in the second through fifth metatarsals and phalanges. Beneath the ulcer, the plantar first metatarsal head shows confluent low T1 marrow signal with high STIR signal and post-contrast enhancement, extending from the plantar cortex, which is thinned and irregular, findings consistent with osteomyelitis of the first metatarsal head. The proximal phalanx of the great toe shows mild reactive edema without low T1 signal. No fracture. No other marrow lesion.

**Other:** Vascular calcification of the digital arteries. No mass.

## Impression

1. Changes in the bone beneath the right plantar first metatarsal head ulcer as described in the findings.
2. Mild first metatarsophalangeal degenerative change with small effusion.
3. No abscess or soft tissue gas.
4. Fatty atrophy of the intrinsic muscles, consistent with neuropathy.

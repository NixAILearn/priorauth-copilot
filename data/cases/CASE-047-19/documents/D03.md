# Riverbend Orthopedics Imaging: MRI Report

**Patient:** Rosalind Ardent · **DOB:** 09/21/1951
**Exam:** MRI RIGHT KNEE without contrast
**Scan date: 09/19/2026**
**Ordering:** Dr. Zeno Thackeray-Ng

## Clinical indication

Knee pain with mechanical symptoms in a patient with osteoarthritis. Evaluate menisci, cartilage and loose bodies.

## Comparison

No prior MRI of this knee is available for comparison.

## Technique

Multiplanar proton density, T2 fat-saturated and T1 images, with no motion artifact, of the right on a 1.5 T system.

## Findings

**Hardware:** Susceptibility artifact from medial compartment arthroplasty components limits assessment of the medial compartment.
**Menisci:** Lateral meniscus with mild degeneration, no discrete tear. Medial meniscus not assessable.
**Ligaments:** ACL, PCL, MCL and LCL intact.
**Extensor mechanism:** Quadriceps and patellar tendons intact.
**Cartilage:** Along the lateral femoral condyle there is a full-thickness cartilage defect with an unstable chondral flap, fluid tracking beneath the flap. Patellofemoral cartilage with mild surface fraying.
**Bone:** Mild subchondral edema of the lateral femoral condyle beneath the cartilage defect. No fracture. No osteonecrosis.
**Joint:** Small effusion. Small popliteal cyst.
**Soft tissues:** Unremarkable. No mass. Popliteal vessels normal in caliber. Pes anserine bursa without fluid.

## Impression

1. Full-thickness lateral femoral condyle cartilage defect with an unstable chondral flap.
2. Medial compartment arthroplasty artifact; lateral compartment osteoarthritis with small joint effusion.

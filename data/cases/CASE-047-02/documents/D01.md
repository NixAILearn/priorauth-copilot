# Riverbend Orthopedics: Office Visit

**Patient:** Umberto Northcote · **DOB:** 01/18/1959 · **Date of visit: 08/06/2026**
**Provider:** Dr. Corinne Jessop, Orthopedic surgeon · **Referring:** Dr. Rosalind Calloway-Reyes

## Chief complaint

Left knee pain with mechanical symptoms.

## HPI

Ms. Northcote is a 67 y/o F with left knee pain for about two years, gradually worse. Pain is medial, aching, worse on stairs and after walking. About three weeks ago she felt a pop getting up from a squat. Since then the knee gets stuck bent and cannot be straightened until it is twisted free, and lately it will not straighten at all. 

Treatment so far: acetaminophen; she has not had therapy for this episode because the knee is mechanically stuck.

Function: walks about 15 to 20 minutes before needing to rest, uses the railing on stairs, and has stopped longer outings with family because of the knee. Sleep is sometimes disturbed by aching after a busy day.

## Imaging

## Assessment

Acutely locked left knee from a displaced bucket-handle meniscal tear, on a background of mild osteoarthritis.

## Plan

1. Arthroscopic debridement of the left knee with treatment of the displaced meniscal fragment, as soon as authorized.
2. Risks reviewed.
3. Request prior authorization from Northwind Health Plan.
4. Crutches and acetaminophen until surgery.

## Discussion

We reviewed the imaging together using a knee model. The patient asked about recovery: most people walk the same day with crutches for a few days, return to light activity within two to three weeks, and continue strengthening exercises afterward. Questions were answered and the patient wishes to proceed.

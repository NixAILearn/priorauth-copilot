# Cedar Hollow Sleep Center: CPAP Titration Study

**Patient:** Nadir Lindqvist-Amari · **DOB:** 03/09/1955 · **Study date:** 02/08/2026
**Interpreting physician:** Dr. Yusra Valcourt

## Procedure

Attended in-laboratory full-night CPAP titration with standard montage. Hypopneas scored with the 4% desaturation rule. CPAP was started at 5 cm H2O and raised for obstructive apneas, hypopneas, snoring or flow limitation per AASM titration guidance.

## Mask and comfort

Medium full face mask fitted by the technologist before lights out. She tolerated CPAP through the night with the full face mask and asked to keep the same mask for home use. Mild mouth leak at higher pressures.

| Pressure | Time at pressure | Residual events |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 cm H2O | 38 min | 52 per hour |
| 7 cm H2O | 46 min | 41 per hour |
| 9 cm H2O | 62 min | 33 per hour |

| 12 cm H2O | 118 min | 22 per hour, supine REM included |
| 13 cm H2O | 31 min | 24 per hour, frequent arousals |
| 15 cm H2O | 22 min | pressure intolerance, lowered |

## Interpretation

Residual AHI at 12 cm H2O: 22.0 events/hour, the best result of the night with supine REM sleep recorded. Higher pressures did not lower events further and caused arousals and leak. Persistent obstructive events on optimal CPAP suggest that CPAP alone will not control her sleep apnea.

## Recommendation

CPAP 12 cm H2O with heated humidity and the full face mask used tonight. Follow up in sleep clinic after 30 to 90 days of home use.

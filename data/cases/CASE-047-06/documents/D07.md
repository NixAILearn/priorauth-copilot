# Lakemont Surgery Center: Pre-anesthesia Evaluation

**Patient:** Kestrel Ardent · **DOB:** 03/04/1952 · **Date:** 07/30/2026
**Anesthesiologist:** Dr. Zeno Underhill · **Planned procedure:** right knee arthroscopy, outpatient

## History

74 y/o F with heart failure with preserved ejection fraction, diagnosed 2023, NYHA class II, on furosemide and losartan, no admissions in the past year. Echo 2025: ejection fraction 60%, mild diastolic dysfunction. Hypertension controlled. Walks one block and climbs one flight of stairs slowly. No chest pain or syncope.

## Exam

BP 132/78, HR 70, SpO2 96% RA. Lungs clear. No edema. Airway: Mallampati II, good mouth opening.

## Assessment

ASA class III. Increased perioperative cardiac risk from heart failure, currently compensated. Assessment: high risk but acceptable for outpatient knee arthroscopy with standard monitoring, regional or general anesthesia, careful fluid management and an extended recovery stay before discharge.

## Plan

Continue furosemide; hold the morning dose on the day of surgery. Continue losartan except the morning of surgery. Arrive early for labs. Discharge home with a family member after recovery criteria are met. Daily weights for a week after surgery, and call the heart clinic for a gain of more than 3 lb.

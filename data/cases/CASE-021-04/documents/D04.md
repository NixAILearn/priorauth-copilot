# Lakemont ENT Associates: Operative Report

**Patient:** Amara Iverholt · **DOB:** 06/14/1961
**Procedure date: 06/02/2026**
**Surgeon:** Dr. Nadir Underhill · **Anesthesia:** monitored anesthesia care, propofol infusion

**Procedure:** Drug-induced sleep endoscopy (DISE), flexible nasopharyngolaryngoscopy under sedation.
**Pre-op dx:** Obstructive sleep apnea (G47.33), intolerance of positive airway pressure.

## Description

Pt positioned supine with a pillow as for sleep. Topical decongestant and anesthetic to the right naris. Propofol titrated to snoring with observed obstructive events. Scope passed without difficulty and several obstructive cycles were watched at each level before grading. Jaw thrust done at the end.

## Findings (VOTE)

Velum: complete anteroposterior collapse with each event, and no complete concentric collapse at the level of the velum.

| Level | Degree | Pattern |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oropharynx (lateral walls) | Partial (1) | Lateral |
| Tongue base | Partial (1) | Anteroposterior |
| Epiglottis | None (0) | n/a |

Tonsils small and not contributing. Nasal cavity patent.

## Complications

None. Recovered in PACU and discharged home with an escort.

## Impression

Multilevel collapse without a concentric pattern at the soft palate. Airway findings are favorable for upper airway stimulation.

## Study type and indication

This was an attended in-laboratory diagnostic polysomnogram. Indication: snoring, witnessed pauses in breathing and daytime sleepiness (Epworth Sleepiness Scale 11/24). No prior sleep study on file.

## Methods

Standard montage: EEG (frontal, central and occipital leads referenced to the contralateral mastoid), bilateral EOG, chin and bilateral anterior tibialis EMG, single-lead ECG, nasal pressure transducer, oronasal thermistor, thoracic and abdominal effort belts, pulse oximetry, body position sensor and synchronized video. A registered technologist attended the full study. Scoring followed current AASM scoring rules; hypopneas scored using the 4% oxygen desaturation rule (a 30% or greater drop in airflow for at least 10 seconds with a desaturation of at least 4%). Apneas were classified by respiratory effort.

# Lakemont ENT Associates: Operative Report

**Patient:** Pell Strathmore · **DOB:** 01/12/1958
**Procedure date: 04/05/2026**
**Surgeon:** Dr. Ines Ostrander · **Anesthesia:** monitored anesthesia care, propofol infusion

**Procedure:** Drug-induced sleep endoscopy (DISE), flexible nasopharyngolaryngoscopy under sedation.
**Pre-op dx:** Obstructive sleep apnea (G47.33), intolerance of positive airway pressure.

## Description

Pt positioned supine with a pillow as for sleep. Topical decongestant and anesthetic to the right naris. Propofol titrated to snoring with observed obstructive events. Scope passed without difficulty and several obstructive cycles were watched at each level before grading. Jaw thrust done at the end. Oxygen saturation was monitored throughout and stayed above safe limits with brief dips during events.

## Findings (VOTE)

Velum: the soft palate showed complete concentric collapse at the level of the velum on every observed cycle, with lateral wall involvement.

| Level | Degree | Pattern |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oropharynx (lateral walls) | Complete (2) | Lateral |
| Tongue base | Partial (1) | Anteroposterior |
| Epiglottis | None (0) | n/a |

Tonsils small and not contributing. Nasal cavity patent.

## Complications

None. Recovered in PACU and discharged home with an escort.

## Impression

Complete concentric collapse at the soft palate. This pattern usually responds poorly to upper airway stimulation; palate surgery may be considered.
